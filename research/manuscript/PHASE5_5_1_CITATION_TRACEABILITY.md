## 1. Traceability Methodology and Evidentiary Categories

In strict compliance with Phase 5.5.1 guidelines, each of the 17 references cited in the manuscript draft was evaluated to determine whether the cited primary source actually supports the specific empirical or conceptual claims made in the manuscript prose.

The audit explicitly distinguishes among five evaluation verdicts:
- **`SUPPORTED`:** The complete primary source (or official consensus standard specification) was directly inspected, and the exact passage, section, or table confirms the asserted claim.
- **`PARTIALLY SUPPORTED`:** The core claim (e.g. mathematical equation, epidemiological overview) is verified via standard reference implementations, abstracts, or official database records, but the full subscription/historic text was not retrieved in full.
- **`UNRESOLVED`:** The claim cannot be independently verified from available primary source materials.
- **`UNSUPPORTED`:** The cited source does not state or directly contradicts the claim made in the manuscript.
