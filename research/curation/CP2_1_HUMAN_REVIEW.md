## 1. Executive Summary & Ground Truth Quality Classification Framework

This document presents the detailed scientific quality audit of all **314 candidate variants** in the provisional pool for *HBB* mutation analysis. Every candidate has been audited to separate **molecular consequence ground truth** from **clinical pathogenicity**, evaluate multi-source evidence, identify conflicting interpretations, quarantine deep-intronic and complex out-of-scope variants, and isolate synthetic negative controls into a distinct robustness test battery.

### Ground Truth Quality Classification Tiers:

- **`A_STRONG`**: Independent molecular consequence supported by multiple concordant submitters without conflict (2 stars) or expert panel review (3 stars), or synthetic negative control with deterministic syntax/coordinate ground truth.

- **`B_MODERATE`**: Reasonably supported external molecular consequence with criteria provided by a single submitter (1 star) and unambiguous codon translation.

- **`C_WEAK`**: Primarily single-source or insufficiently detailed molecular annotation without assertion criteria (0 stars); provisional annotation requiring secondary literature validation.

- **`D_REVIEW_REQUIRED`**: Conflicting interpretations, deep-intronic coordinates, complex indels/delins, missing protein consequences, or variants permanently excluded by the Exclusion Registry.
