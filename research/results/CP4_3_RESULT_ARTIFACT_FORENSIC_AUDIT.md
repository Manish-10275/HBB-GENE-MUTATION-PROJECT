## 2. Forensic Investigation of the Suspected Discrepancy

### 2.1 The Historical Flaw (Pre-CP4 Execution)
In the initial execution of Phase 2C (prior to October 8, 2026), the runner script evaluated:
```python
# Old pre-CP4 logic in run_phase2c_benchmark.py (Lines 128-144)
elif eng_mol == exp_mol and eng_prot == exp_prot:
    comparison_cat = "EXACT_MOLECULAR_AGREEMENT"
    primary_status = "MATCH"
    protein_status = "MATCH"
```
Because `exp_mol` and `exp_prot` were pre-calculated by the engine during candidate pool generation:
- Runtime output $A$ matched $B$ in 55/55 records.
- The script reported 55/55 `EXACT_MOLECULAR_AGREEMENT` and 55/55 `MATCH` for protein consequence.
- External ClinVar evidence ($C$) was extracted into variables `ext_mol` and `ext_prot`, but was **never evaluated in the conditional logic**.
- Because ClinVar stores 1-letter IUPAC notation (e.g. `N109K`, `E7fs`, `Q128*`) or empty strings, raw $A$ equaled raw $C$ in **0 of 55** records.

This circularity was uncovered during an internal audit and classified as `RED — BENCHMARK COMPARISON INVALID` in [`PHASE2C_COMPARISON_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`](file:///e:/HBB%20GENE%20MUTATION%20PROJECT/research/results/PHASE2C_COMPARISON_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md).

### 2.2 The CP4 Architecture Correction
In response, Checkpoint CP4 instituted the **Three-Way Data Model**:
- **Field $A$ (Runtime Engine Output):** Generated at benchmark runtime by executing `parse_hgvs()` and `evaluate_consequence()`.
- **Field $B$ (Frozen Expected Deterministic Output):** Retained strictly for software regression testing ($A == B$). Quarantined to prevent oracle leakage.
- **Field $C$ (External ClinVar Evidence):** The sole active external reference for biological consequence comparison ($A$ vs $C$).

The comparison logic was extracted into an independent, tested module: [`research/curation/phase2c_comparison_engine.py`](file:///e:/HBB%20GENE%20MUTATION%20PROJECT/research/curation/phase2c_comparison_engine.py), which implements IUPAC 1-to-3 letter normalization, semantic synonymous translation, and explicit handling of truncated frameshifts and non-coding variants.

When executed in Checkpoint CP4.3, this corrected engine produced the current result set:
- 30 `EXACT_MOLECULAR_AGREEMENT`
- 20 `MOLECULAR_AGREEMENT_PROTEIN_DIFFERENCE`
- 5 `EXTERNAL_ANNOTATION_INCOMPLETE`
- 28 protein `MATCH`, 20 `DISCREPANCY`, 5 `INCOMPLETE`, 2 `NOT_APPLICABLE`

Therefore, the suspected discrepancy arose from comparing the **remediated post-CP4.3 reports against the remembered characteristics of the pre-CP4 flawed execution**, rather than an actual mismatch in the repository's current artifacts.

## 4. Field Provenance and Data Model Analysis

To maintain research integrity, the provenance and role of each comparison field are explicitly defined:

### 4.1 Field A: Runtime Deterministic Engine Output
- **Fields in JSON:** `runtime_molecular_consequence`, `runtime_protein_consequence`, `runtime_engine_output`.
- **Provenance:** Computed on-the-fly by executing `parse_hgvs(hgvs)` and `evaluate_consequence(variant, ref_seq)` against local reference sequences (`NM_000518.5` and `NG_000007.3`).
- **Format:** Fully formatted HGVS nomenclature (e.g. `Missense`, `p.Asn109Lys`; `Frameshift`, `p.Glu7ArgfsTer13`).

### 4.2 Field B: Pre-Computed Expected Deterministic Consequence
- **Fields in JSON:** `expected_molecular_consequence`, `expected_protein_consequence`, `frozen_expected_deterministic`.
- **Provenance:** Stored in `phase2c_primary.json` under `deterministic_molecular_consequence` and `deterministic_protein_consequence`. Generated during candidate pool construction by running the consequence engine.
- **Strict Role:** **Software regression invariant ONLY** ($A == B$).
- **Integrity Rule:** Field $B$ does **not** constitute independent biological evidence. It is strictly quarantined within `software_regression_check` and **never** influences `primary_endpoint_status`, `protein_endpoint_status`, or `comparison_category`.

### 4.3 Field C: External ClinVar Biological Annotation
- **Fields in JSON:** `external_molecular_annotation_raw`, `external_protein_annotation_raw`, `external_clinvar_evidence`.
- **Provenance:** Extracted directly from NCBI ClinVar Variation archives under `NM_000518.5`.
- **Format:** Submitter-provided notations, typically 1-letter IUPAC (e.g. `N109K`, `V134L`), legacy stop notations (`Q128*`), truncated frameshifts (`E7fs`), or absent strings (`""`).
- **Strict Role:** The **sole active reference** for biological concordance evaluation ($A$ vs $C$).
